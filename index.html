02Full-Stack MERN · Deployed on Vercel

GHM — Pharmacy Management & Billing System

An end-to-end operations platform for a medical shop. It brings point-of-sale billing, batch-wise inventory with expiry tracking, purchases, vendor & customer ledgers and a live profit & loss dashboard into one secure web app, replacing manual registers with accurate, real-time data.

My role

Designed and developed the product end to end: MongoDB data modelling, a secure Express REST API, the React 19 interface and cloud deployment. My accounting background shaped the ledgers, GST billing and P&L logic.

  • POS billing & GST invoices

    Fast cart-based checkout with GST, discounts, payment modes and one-click PDF bills.

  • Batch & expiry tracking

    First-Expiry-First-Out stock allocation across batches, with pack + loose-unit selling (strips & tablets).

  • Live business dashboard

    Revenue trends, top products, low-stock and expiring-soon alerts, plus a full profit & loss statement.

  • Purchases & ledgers

    Vendor purchases, supplier and customer credit ledgers, expenses and a daily day book.

  • Role-based security

    JWT authentication with hashed passwords and Admin / Staff permissions on every route.

  • Reports & data import

    Excel and PDF report exports, bulk product import and Cloudinary image uploads.
